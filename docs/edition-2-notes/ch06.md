### Coordinator stop before correction round 1: join or wake-any

After the consolidated initial review, the student was assigned correction round

parent waits for both agents and receives both completions in one wake. §6.8

Two distinct reliable contracts fit parts of that description:

- Join an explicitly submitted set: both Agents execute concurrently, and one
  parent result returns their identity-tagged completions after both finish.
