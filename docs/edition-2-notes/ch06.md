## Interrupt lifetime: resolved by Bill

This is the consequential contradiction already recorded in the carryover:

- TL;DR and §6.5 (lines 540–543) stop the actor on interrupt and discard later
  tool results delivered to its stopped mailbox.
- §6.7 (lines 677–684) ends only the active turn, keeps the actor receiving
  messages, and records real results from Go handlers that still finish.
  Explicit job kill has separate semantics and suppresses that killed job's
  late output. The revised shutdown paragraphs also preserve real outcomes.

The coordinator originally recommended following §6.7 and stopped for Bill.
Interrupt should end the active turn while leaving the Agent usable; preserve
actual results from jobs that were not explicitly killed. This fits the chapter's
distinction between interrupt, job kill and shutdown. The future author should
remove the contradictory early prose and pseudocode during the later author phase.

Bill has now agreed: "interrupt let's e talk to teh agent starting a new turn,
but other jobs continue." The ruling is that interrupt ends the current turn,
leaves the Agent usable for a new turn, and does not kill its other jobs. Preserve
their real results. Explicit job kill and shutdown remain distinct operations.
The mandatory skill and carryover now carry this decision. Chapter 6 can proceed;
the original first-edition chapter remains unchanged during the student run.
