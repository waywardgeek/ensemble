Date: 2026-10-07. Status: execution-guide draft accepted after independent
proofreading and workflow review. This is not Bill's editorial approval or

Read the complete first-edition and second-edition Chapter 0, the new outline
and evidence record, the current full voice guide, and the current full
chapter-writing procedure. The new Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 contracts were

The opening excerpt matches the original account. The guide removes the
unsupported universal promises that any model will score 100, that a newer
model necessarily produces better code, and that a passing score completes
the chapter. It reconciles the former code-first procedure with the current
teach/build/check/live-run/compare/revise/proofread sequence.

The ordinary reader has a direct path to Chapter 1 without a required
publishing team. Optional worker roles remain distinct, with explicit phase
scheduling and no guarantee about host slot limits or persistent workers.
The sample handoff includes the full mandatory skill read, reload after
compaction, architecture and complete contracts, fresh second-edition history,
the cold-build instruction boundary, questions before affected code, and
sanitized all-feature live evidence. Human answers must be relayed and
recorded; elapsed time is not consent.

The architecture summary preserves ownership, the common vocabulary versus
runtime Ensemble distinction, behavior placement, logger reachability,
public clients, and the separate optional GUI module. The execution guide
preserves independent post-run comparison with the old standard, subsequent
coder/author correction, review of the revision, and separate initial and
validated checkpoints. It does not mistake a GUI stub for a browser run or
a local fault for a provider failure.

The durable-record instructions distinguish sources from summaries and
explicitly reject a guarantee that the entire textbook remains in context.
Credential guidance is appropriate for readers using their own authorized
sources and does not publish the author's local secrets or depend on his
configuration file.

No material revision was requested. The author's scoped prose lint is a
separate check. The coordinator subsequently removed the manuscript's
verification marker because its historical external figures are not reused.
The verification requirement remains in the evidence record before any
future reuse; the new guide makes no fresh benchmark claim. Chapter 0 adds no program or live
demonstration requiring a fabricated execution receipt.
