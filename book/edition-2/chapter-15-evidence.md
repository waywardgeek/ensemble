useful test-design lessons:

- Oldest and newest are equivalent if a fold consumes every available entry.
  Seed a genuinely distinguishing selected prefix and survivor in the new check.

  present-entry comparison. Edit while the prior version is represented too.

  source band needed to test a later graduation failure. That remained an open
