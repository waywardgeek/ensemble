# Chapter 29: Puffin

> **Design-stage outline.** This chapter is not an implemented or graded
> construction assignment. Its detailed contracts and exercises are still being
> developed. Do not infer deployment authority or completed capabilities from it.

## In Plain Words

Assemble a family assistant from shared infrastructure rather than build a one-off platform.

## Planned construction

- Preserve identity and memories with provenance; do not import stale permissions or scheduled work.
- Fresh, replaceable schedule context backed by Agenda.
- Explicit connection grants and Linux command confinement before shell access.
- Synthetic end-to-end reminders and briefings before separately authorized live effects.
- Lean ARM64 deployment on xyzzy, backup restoration, and deliberate retirement of old scheduling authority.
