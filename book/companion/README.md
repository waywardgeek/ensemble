# The Art of Building AI Coding Agents — A Human Companion

A book for experienced software engineers who want the ideas, mechanisms, tradeoffs, and hard-won lessons without executing the specifications.

This is the replacement companion for the C# edition of the agentic codebook. It is written to stand on its own. Each chapter links to its implementation chapter for exact contracts, examples, and exercises; the companion concentrates on why the design works and what went wrong along the way.
