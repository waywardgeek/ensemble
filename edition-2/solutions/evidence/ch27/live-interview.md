# Ensemble workflow interview

## Friction points

What slows you down most in your coding workflow?

The biggest slowdown is missing context: unclear requirements, unfamiliar code paths, and no reliable way to run tests. A focused task, relevant files, and fast test feedback make the biggest difference.

What burns the most tokens in your work?

Repeatedly reading large files, verbose tool output, and revisiting the same context after failed attempts. Targeted searches, small diffs, and concise test results help keep token use down.

Where do you need a human's help?

Most useful: clarifying ambiguous requirements, choosing between product trade-offs, and approving risky changes. I also need someone with access to provide missing credentials or environment details and verify behavior I can’t test directly.

## Improvement opportunities

A code-navigation tool that returns the smallest relevant context: definitions, callers, related tests, and dependencies. That would cut down on file reading and help me make better-targeted changes.
