Create a wide editorial header illustration for a technical paper titled
"Knowledge Patches: Maintaining Software by Maintaining What Builds It."
Landscape composition, approximately 3:1, suitable for the top of a document.
Do not render the title or any other text inside the image.

The central subject is a large, open engineering codebook. A human engineer and
a small, abstract AI collaborator work beside it, examining a discovered flaw
and adding one clear correction to a page. Make their shared attention and
exchange of understanding visible: the human is an active participant.

From the book, two broad, easy-to-follow paths extend toward the right. Along
the upper path, the correction repairs an existing modular software structure.
Along the lower path, a fresh builder follows the revised book and assembles
a second working structure from separate pieces. Both structures satisfy the
same visible functional pattern, but need not have identical internal shapes.
Use simple architectural blocks to represent software, without miniature code.

A clear return arrow carries a discovered lesson from the building work back
into an earlier page of the book. The book is visibly the enduring source of
knowledge; the software structures are its reproducible products. Integrate the
correction into the page itself rather than depicting an accumulating pile of
patches stuck onto the finished software.

Style: thoughtful technical editorial illustration, crisp geometric forms,
subtle paper texture, restrained depth, generous white space. White or warm-white
background, dark charcoal outlines, deep blue for construction paths, and one
warm amber accent for the new lesson. Strong contrast and large readable shapes
for a reader with low vision. The composition must remain understandable in
grayscale and at document-header size. Favor one coherent scene over a crowded
infographic. Avoid tiny labels, fake code, decorative circuitry, glowing brains,
humanoid robots, logos, watermarks, and claims of effortless or perfect automation.
