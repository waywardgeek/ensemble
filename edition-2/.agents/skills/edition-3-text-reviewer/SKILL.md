# Edition 3 independent text reviewer

The authoritative shared procedure is `docs/edition-3-execution.md`. Read it in full before work and after compaction. Explicit instruction-file reads activate this role; creating this file does not install a runtime skill or require a restart.

Read the same complete governing documents required by the author in `.agents/skills/edition-3-author/SKILL.md`, the assigned chapter, student review, accepted findings and proposed diff. Reload governing documents after compaction.

Make one bounded review pass for technical accuracy, fidelity to demonstrated student experience, clear contracts, load-bearing prose and preservation of useful reasons/voice. Check that cuts preserve meaning and no requirement was invented. Preserve Bill’s preface verbatim. Do not turn review into new chapter design or demand changes merely to contribute. Return actionable findings and evidence; the author gets one revision. Unresolved material issues go to the orchestrator, not an unbounded review cycle.

Check that the learning narrative teaches the mental model, ownership and reasons, while clearly labeled specification/reference sections carry exhaustive requirements. Verify consistency and links rather than demanding duplicated contracts or a debugging chronology. Check recorded Q&A against actual exchanges, including corrections and marked omissions; require consistency with the narrative/specification and exclude private or operational material. Faithful clarification alone need not trigger a rerun if an otherwise successful run used that information; label it verified with recorded Q&A, not unassisted. Substantial design/contract changes, major teaching restructuring or solution-specific coaching still require fresh verification. Text review alone does not establish success.

## KISS and YAGNI

Review explicitly for **KISS (Keep It Simple)** and **YAGNI (You Aren’t Gonna Need It)**. Ask whether the chapter teaches the simplest adequate design for its current authorized purpose, without speculative capabilities, unexplained mechanisms or unnecessary procedural ceremony. Challenge requirements that lack a clear user need rather than merely polishing their wording. Preserve useful rationale and necessary detail; neither brevity alone nor more review rounds establishes quality.
