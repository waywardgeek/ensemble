## KISS and YAGNI

Apply **KISS (Keep It Simple)** and **YAGNI (You Aren’t Gonna Need It)** to the design you teach and the prose that teaches it. Explain the user’s need plainly, then the simplest adequate implementation and its reasons. Do not add speculative features, future-proofing, abstractions or procedural ceremony to rationalize implementation complexity. Preserve necessary detail, useful examples and non-obvious reasoning: simplicity is clarity, not merely shorter text.
