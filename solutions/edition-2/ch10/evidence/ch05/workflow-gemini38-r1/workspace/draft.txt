We warmly welcome you to the Oakridge Public Library this Saturday at 10 a.m. for our community Repair Cafe, where repairs are free. Skilled volunteer fixers will be on hand to help you troubleshoot small household appliances, mend clothing, and restore beloved items. Feel free to bring a broken lamp, torn sweater, or stubborn toaster and learn practical repair skills alongside your neighbors. Admission is free, and refreshments will be provided while you wait for your turn at a workbench.
